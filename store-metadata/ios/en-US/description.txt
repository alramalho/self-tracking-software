tracking.so pairs an AI coach with the people who keep you going.

Tell the coach what you want to change, like running your first half marathon, practicing guitar or reading before bed. It turns that goal into a week of sessions you review and adjust. Your friends see the effort along the way.

AN AI COACH THAT PLANS YOUR WEEK
• Describe your goal in your own words, and your coach suggests a first week for you to review
• Weekly reviews look at what you actually did and adjust the next week to fit
• When a goal goes quiet, your coach checks in with a short, kind note, and you choose: pick it back up or let it go
• Chat any time: "Hard week, should we make it lighter?"

ACCOUNTABILITY WITH FRIENDS
• Follow friends and see their latest activities, photos and streaks
• React and comment to cheer each other on
• Join Circles: small groups working on similar things, where everyone keeps their own plan
• You choose what to share. Private notes stay private

APPLE HEALTH, MATCHED FOR YOU
• Connect Apple Health and tracking.so finds your new workouts
• It suggests the activity each workout belongs to, and you confirm with a tap. Nothing is logged behind your back
• See heart rate, route and effort for your workouts
• Apple Health data is never used for advertising

LOG IN SECONDS
• Log from iPhone, from Apple Watch, or just say what you did with a voice note
• Add photos, a quick note and how hard it felt
• Weekly streaks show your consistency, and a missed week stays visible so you can get back on track

BRING YOUR OWN CURRICULUM
Already learning with another AI agent? Connect it over MCP to keep your curriculum, schedule and weekly status in sync, while your tracking.so coach stays focused on accountability.

FREE TO START
Tracking, streaks and friends are free. AI coaching is an optional subscription.
